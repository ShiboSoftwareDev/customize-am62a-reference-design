# Reference connector models

`beagleplay-hdmi.glb` contains the `HDMI19P_1D155_23_1X7_7X16_4MM` product from BeagleBoard’s [official BeaglePlay STEP release](https://github.com/beagleboard/beagleplay/blob/7a59a98ae27dc4fd9e2bd8975ff90cdb44a366ea/Design/beagleplayv10_a2_board_3D_20221219.stp). The original BREP, assembly placement, and materials are retained. This is the narrow through-hole HDMI connector used by J4, rather than the horizontal surface-mount Amphenol connector used by SK-AM62.

Regenerate it with `bun scripts/prebuild-beagleplay-connector-model.ts`, then run `bun run prebuild:ti-evm-cad-scenes`. The script downloads the pinned release, extracts this product and its child assembly, and uses `circuit-json-to-gltf` to tessellate it. Temporary STEP sources stay under `tmp/`.

The generated asset uses the exporter’s scene coordinates. Its enrichment mapping restores the package frame and registers the 19 numbered contacts against the imported plated holes. The cached scene tests verify that all 19 leads cross the PCB at their own hole centers.

The dimensioned FPC models use the following package references:

- [TE 1-1734248-5](https://www.te.com/en/product-1-1734248-5.html): vertical, 15 positions at 1 mm, 22.4 × 4.6 × 7.7 mm.
- [GCT FFC2A32](https://gct.co/connector/ffc2a32): vertical, 40 positions at 0.5 mm, 4.25 mm profile; the 40-position housing is 27.4 × 3.3 mm.
- [Molex 5019512230](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/5019512230): vertical V-Flip, 22 positions at 0.5 mm, 4.05 mm profile.
- [Hirose FH12A-40S-0.5SH(55)](https://www.hirose.com/en/product/p/CL0586-0557-8-55): horizontal top-contact, 24.5 × 7 × 2 mm.
- BeaglePlay's 0.5K-DX-40PWB and 0.5K-DX-22PWB use the 25.4 × 5.25 × 2 mm and 15.4 × 5.25 × 2 mm bodies recorded in its official Altium footprint names and BOM.

These are dimensioned JSCAD representations with simplified housings and spring contacts. Solder tails follow every imported SMT land in the package's local frame, including mounting tabs and the TE part's paired A/B tails. They are not tessellations of manufacturer STEP models. Only the prebuilt GLB scenes include this enrichment; electrical and PCB/schematic artifacts are unchanged.

The microSD representation keeps a simplified hollow shell and eight internal mating contacts, rather than drawing contacts on the lid. Signal, card-detect and mounting tails follow the imported SMT lands, including the socket's package-axis offset on BeaglePlay. MEM2051 housing orientation follows the numbered land axis so mirrored bottom-side footprints retain the card-entry direction. The shell is an illustration, not an exact manufacturer model; [GCT's MEM2051 drawing](https://www.farnell.com/cad/2343030.pdf) distinguishes internal mating contacts from the PCB solder areas.

The surface-mount HDMI models register their numbered signal contacts against the imported lands. TMDS62LEVM J13 uses a dimensioned JAE DC04S019JA1 model based on [drawing SJ120821](https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/913/DOC041913213.pdf). TI's released assembly uses an extruded body for J13. The JSCAD replacement models the 15 × 10.25 × 6.1 mm shell, two staggered mating-contact rows, 19 solder tails, and both pairs of shield tabs. It omits the shell spring cutouts and stamping details. The micro-HDMI reference uses KiCad's [Molex 46765-0x01 footprint](https://github.com/KiCad/kicad-footprints/blob/master/Connector_HDMI.pretty/HDMI_Micro-D_Molex_46765-0x01.kicad_mod), with footprint Y reversed into STEP coordinates. The prebuilt-scene regression checks all 19 solder tails against their lands on both mounting sides.

Symmetric chip passives align their long axis through the imported land centers. Crystal package heights, when present in the footprint name, include the substrate rather than being added above it.

BeaglePlay SW1–SW3 use a side-actuated TS23M representation, with the 4.55 × 2.3 mm body from the Altium footprint and the 1.85 mm height from the [official schematic](https://docs.beagleboard.org/beagleplay.pdf). The actuator projects toward package +Y, toward the board edge, and the five solder contacts follow the imported lands. The official STEP assembly's switch product has no solid geometry, so this is a simplified dimensioned model.

BeagleBone Black C34 uses the [CDE AVE case-D drawing](https://www.cde.com/resources/catalogs/AVE.pdf): a 6.3 mm metal can, 6.6 mm square base, 5.3 mm seated height and two 2.7 × 0.65 mm solder tails. The chamfered base and negative marking follow the package's polarity, with pin 1 on local -X. Lettering and internal construction are omitted.

TMDS62LEVM and SK-AM62 use Würth's exact 629104151021 USB-A and 629105150521 micro-USB STEP models from the manufacturer's [pinned KiCad library](https://github.com/WurthElektronik/KiCad-Library/tree/215595ffc7c98649394598bc6bac81ddc421fcc6). Numbered solder lands in the matching footprints define the package datum: USB-A pins are at STEP Y=6.75 mm, and micro-USB pins are at Y=2.9 mm. Registration reverses KiCad footprint Y into the STEP frame and matches the imported pin numbers. This replaces centering generic housings on imported bounding boxes, which put their rear walls through neighboring parts. Tests check distinct solder-tail patches on all six connectors and the previously intersected neighbors.

The 3D assembly omits co-layout alternatives that cannot be fitted together. TI's `PROC181E1-1_BOM.xls` in the [matching design archive](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/sprcal9) marks L2/L9 Fitted and R181/R185/R266/R271 Not Fitted. BeaglePlay's [released BOM](https://github.com/beagleboard/beagleplay/blob/7a59a98ae27dc4fd9e2bd8975ff90cdb44a366ea/BeaglePlay_bom.csv) fits C1 and L15/L20, omitting R221 and R219/R225/R264/R265. Its Altium schematic explicitly notes the C1/R221 co-layout. The older Altium export and released BOM differ in some part values and package revisions; this population choice does not substitute the newer package dimensions or alter the imported PCB, schematic, or connectivity.

The complete 3D population exclusions are stored in `not-fitted-component-names.json` beside the prebuilder. TMDS62LEVM uses every `Not Fitted` row of `PROC181E1-1_BOM.xls` in `sprcal9` (archive SHA-256 `40e6c4d0bea5381bf7b4e0ef26ec4ec9adae156be308e4a3838bd344972b7615`); SK-AM62 uses every zero-quantity `DNI` row of `E3 revision/Bill Of Materials/PROC114E3_BOM.xlsx` in [sprr448](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/sprr448). BeaglePlay also omits C254: its native RMII/SPE schematic explicitly co-locates it with the fitted R220, and the pinned released BOM fits only R220. These are CAD population choices; imported source/PCB components and their connections remain unchanged.

BeagleBone Black's `XTAL150SMD_125X196` land dimensions are not the crystal body dimensions or its long-axis direction. Y2 is TXC 7A-24.000MAAJ-T (5 × 3.2 mm, 1.35 mm maximum height); Y3 is Abracon ABM3-25.000MHZ-B2-T (5 × 3.2 × 1.3 mm). Their bodies follow the line through the two land centers, including bottom-side mirroring. Y1 is Citizen CM200C-32.768KAZF-UT (7.9 × 3.7 × 2.5 mm), a plastic four-terminal package. These dimensions and MPNs are recorded in the native Altium schematic; the [TXC drawing](https://www.txccrystal.com/images/pdf/7a.pdf) distinguishes the 5 × 3.2 mm case from the recommended lands. The representations omit lettering and internal construction.

`beagleplay-microsd.glb` extracts the ST-TF-003A socket from the same pinned official STEP release as the HDMI model. It excludes the removable `MicroSD` card product and preserves the socket's original BREP, materials and mounting features. Its nine numbered tails register against the imported lands; the generic centered base previously extended into the nearby protection parts. Regenerate both BeaglePlay connector assets with `bun scripts/prebuild-beagleplay-connector-model.ts`.

`linkpp-lpjg16314a4nl.glb` uses the STEP archive linked on [LINK-PP's LPJG16314A4NL product page](https://www.link-pp.com/products/2632.html). Regenerate with `bun scripts/prebuild-linkpp-connector-model.ts`. The manufacturer archive includes an example PCB: only `TRJG163XXA4NL` is extracted, preserving the actual housing and underside rather than its bounding box. The STEP product's Y=-6.75 mm plane is the seated plane; its ten signal pins register against the TI plated-hole pattern. The manufacturer model uses source Y for height and source Z for depth. Raw STEP files remain temporary; only generated GLB assets and scenes are committed.

The two-terminal ESD packages use the [TI DPL/DPY package drawings](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd1e01b04.pdf), rather than the generic DFN model's 1 mm body and center-pad defaults. DPL uses a nominal 0.6 × 0.3 mm body, 0.32 mm seated envelope and 0.35 mm contact pitch. DPY uses a 1 × 0.6 mm body and 0.65 mm contact pitch; the TI footprints specify 0.5 mm height and BeaglePlay specifies 0.45 mm. Two underside metal rectangles represent the terminals, aligned with the imported land axis on either board side. The DPL pin-one corner detail, lettering and internal construction are omitted.

Dimensioned inductor footprints supply the seated package height, including their terminations. In particular, TMDS62LEVM's XAL7070 uses 7 mm and SK-AM62's XAL6060 uses 6.1 mm, consistent with the maximum envelopes in Coilcraft's [XAL7070](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/1ba55433-bcc8-4838-9b21-382f497e12e0/xal7070.pdf) and [XAL60xx](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/ea51f14b-7f32-4dc6-8dfe-d4b70549040f/xal60xx.pdf) drawings. The simplified ferrite case and contacts remain; height is no longer derived from the package width when the source specifies it.

Multi-terminal SON/DFN/QFN packages with a dimensioned footprint use its body envelope and seated height. Simplified underside contacts follow the imported package-local lands and are clipped to the case; they do not copy the longer PCB solder lands or add a default center pad. Polygon connection anchors supply positions only, with 0.15 mm illustrative contacts where the converter has no physical pad dimensions. Packages without authored height retain their existing library models. The [Microchip AT24C512C UDFN drawing](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/20006161B.pdf) distinguishes the 0.6 mm maximum package from the previous 1 mm DFN default.

The two DCU VSSOP-8 parts use KiCad's 2.3 × 2 mm, 0.5 mm-pitch STEP package, registered by eight numbered contacts, consistent with the [TI SN74LVC3G17 DCU drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc3g17.pdf). Würth's 150040/150080 indicator LEDs are recognized before inductor designators, including TI's `LD` names. Their simplified pale representations of unlit water-clear lenses use nominal 0.25 mm and 0.7 mm heights from the [0402](https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/150040AS73220.pdf) and [0805](https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/150080VS75000.pdf) drawings, without simulated illumination or polarity lettering. DLW21 chokes use four isolated electrodes and a 2 × 1.2 mm body, retaining the source footprint's 1.4 mm maximum seated height (nominal 1.2 ± 0.2 mm) from [Murata's drawing](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/4592525.pdf); continuous end bars would join separate windings visually.

BeaglePlay J18's product in the official STEP release identifies the connector as SM04B-SRSS. The enrichment uses KiCad's pinned JST SH SM04B-SRSS-TB STEP model, including the open housing, four signal tails and two hold-downs. The four 1 mm-pitch signal contacts register against the native footprint; the newer released BOM's substitute part does not replace the older source package dimensions. Raw STEP sources stay temporary.

The SON6 `_PB_0-6` footprint on SK-AM62 U89 and TMDS62LEVM U84 retains its 0.6 mm seated height and six imported terminals, without adding a generic DFN center pad. [TI's TPD4E004 DRY drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd4e004.pdf) specifies the six-terminal package and 0.6 mm maximum height.

SK-AM62 SW3/SW4 use the fitted Bourns 7914G-1-000E: a square 4.5 mm housing, 5 mm base, 2.39 mm round actuator and four 0.76 mm gull-wing leads. The 2.54 mm lid plane and standard 1.65 mm actuator give 4.19 mm seated height, following [Bourns' drawing](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/7914.pdf). The first dimensions in its TI footprint name describe pad spacing, not the case. The model simplifies the cover seams and omits lettering.

SK-AM62 C1 uses the fitted Murata GJM1555C1H2R4BB01D's nominal 0.5 mm height, from the [manufacturer-authored sheet](https://datasheet.octopart.com/GJM1555C1H2R4BB01D-Murata-datasheet-14406022.pdf). Unverified capacitor MPNs retain the existing illustrative package dimensions; footprint maximum heights are not substituted for fitted-part dimensions.

TMDS62LEVM C146/C147 are the fitted UT107M050HFKTA aluminum electrolytics in `PROC181E1-1_BOM.xls`, rather than tantalum capacitors. Their dimensioned can model uses the [UT series drawing](https://www.nextgencomponent.com/uploads/soft/20230707/1-230FG3541Q94.pdf): 8 mm diameter, 8.3 mm base, 10.2 mm nominal seated height and a 9 mm terminal span. Lettering, vent detail and internal construction are omitted.

BeaglePlay's four-land `L2012` footprints are common-mode chokes, not two-terminal inductors. They reuse the four-electrode DLW21 model. The native schematic specifies DLW21SN900HQ2L; [Murata's sheet](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/281/DLW21SN900HQ2_23-1915188.pdf) gives a 2 × 1.2 mm body and 1.2 ± 0.2 mm height. The model uses the 1.4 mm maximum envelope. BeaglePlay B1 uses the ceramic 0900PC15A0036 balun's [Johanson drawing](https://www.johansontechnology.com/datasheets/0900PC15A0036/0900PC15A0036.pdf), with a 2 × 1.25 × 1 mm envelope, ten wraparound terminals and an orientation mark. Its numbered terminals register against the imported bottom-side lands; it has no DFN center pad.

BeagleBone Black S1–S3 use the native KMR231GLFS package, represented by KiCad's pinned `SW_Push_1P1T_NO_CK_KMR2.step`. Its 4.6 × 2.8 × 1.9 mm lead envelope follows the [C&K KMR2 drawing](https://www.ckswitches.com/media/1479/kmr2.pdf). Four reference contact centers register the STEP model against the four imported switch lands. Raw STEP files remain temporary.
